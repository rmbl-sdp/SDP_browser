Welcome to the SDP Browser

Discover, explore, and extract geospatial data from the RMBL Spatial Data Platform — a curated collection of high-resolution datasets for western Colorado.

🔍 Discover Search and filter datasets by domain, type, resolution, and more. Click Discover layers to browse the full catalog.
🗺️ Explore Add layers to the map with dynamic visualization. Scrub through time-series, adjust colormaps, and switch band combinations.
📥 Extract Draw an Area of Interest to get zonal statistics, download GeoTIFF/PNG subsets, or generate ready-to-run R and Python notebooks.
🔗 Share Every view is encoded in the URL. Copy the address bar to share your exact map state with collaborators.
